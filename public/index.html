Questions

Straight answers.

What exactly is in one box?

Each OG box holds 28 individually sealed sachets — one for every day of a 4-week month. Each sachet is a single, pre-measured serve.

When should I take it?

Within 30 minutes after training is ideal. On rest days, take it with breakfast or as a mid-day snack to keep your protein intake consistent.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Absolutely. OG is designed for anyone who trains — from your first month in the gym to your tenth year. Consistency matters more than experience.

How does the subscription work?

The Grinder plan ships a fresh box every 28 days so you never run out. Pause, skip, swap flavours or cancel any time from your account.

How fast does it ship?

Orders are dispatched within 48 hours. You'll get tracking the moment your box leaves the warehouse.
