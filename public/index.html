Questions

Straight answers.

What's in one box?

Each OG protein box holds 28 single-serve sachets of 34g — one for every day of a 4-week month. Every sachet carries 24g of protein.

Whey or fermented yeast protein — which one is for me?

OG Whey Protein Concentrate is a dairy-based protein, available in Swiss Choco and Vanilla. OG Fermented Yeast Protein is a dairy-free protein source, available in Choco Berry and Vanilla. Both give 24g of protein per sachet.

Where do you deliver and how fast?

We deliver across India. Orders to Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Kerala reach you in 2–3 days; everywhere else within 7 days. Both are subject to availability. You can check your pincode in the Delivery section above.

What's in OG Creatine + Taurine?

A 120g tub of creatine monohydrate with taurine, available in Fruit Fusion and Orange. It's made to improve muscle strength, support cognitive function and boost performance (as stated on the pack). Follow the serving size and directions on the label.

Do you offer cash on delivery?

No. All orders are prepaid online via UPI, debit/credit card or netbanking through a secure payment gateway. Your order is confirmed the moment payment succeeds.
