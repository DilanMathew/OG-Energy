Shipping & delivery

  • We currently deliver only within Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Kerala.
  • Orders are typically delivered within 2–3 days of successful payment, subject to stock availability and courier serviceability at your pincode.
  • You can track your order at any time on the Track order page using your order ID and mobile number.

Payments

  • All orders are 100% prepaid. We do not offer cash on delivery.
  • Payments are processed securely by our payment gateway partner via UPI, debit/credit cards and netbanking. We never see or store your card details.
  • Your order is confirmed only after the payment is successfully received.

Cancellation & refunds

  • You may request cancellation before your order is shipped by contacting us with your order ID. Approved cancellations are refunded in full.
  • For hygiene and safety reasons, opened or used nutritional products cannot be returned.
  • If you receive a damaged, incorrect or expired product, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order ID and photos; we will replace it or refund you.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method and usually reflect within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.
  • If money was debited but your order was not confirmed, it is refunded automatically by the payment gateway.

Privacy

We collect your name, mobile number, email and delivery address only to process, deliver and support your order. We share these details only with our payment and delivery partners for that purpose. We do not sell your data. The website stores your cart and checkout details in your own browser for convenience.
